Classic Waffles


Back to Home

Description:

This recipe is a simple way to make delicious, homemade waffles using basic ingredients like flour, eggs, and milk. To prepare them, you just mix the dry and wet ingredients separately, combine them into a smooth batter, and cook them in a waffle iron until they are golden brown. These waffles are crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, making them a much better and more filling meal than store-bought options.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt or to taste
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 ½ cups warm milk
  • 1 ⅓ cup butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
